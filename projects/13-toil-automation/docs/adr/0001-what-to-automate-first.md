# ADR-0001: What to automate first

- **Status:** Proposed
- **Date:**
- **Deciders:**

## Context

Everyone has a favourite thing to automate. Without data, you automate what's interesting instead of what costs the most.

## Options considered

### Option 1: Rank by hours/month
- **Pros:** Maximises time saved.
- **Cons:** Ignores risk: a rare task done wrong can cost more than a frequent one.

### Option 2: Rank by hours/month × risk of doing it wrong manually
- **Pros:** Captures both cost and danger (e.g. the pre-market check: short but critical).
- **Cons:** "Risk" is a judgement; write down how you scored it.

### Option 3: Rank by ease of automation
- **Pros:** Quick wins build momentum.
- **Cons:** Easy things are often low-value.

## Questions to answer before deciding

- What did two weeks of measurement (or your game days) actually show?
- Which item would hurt most if done late on a busy morning?
