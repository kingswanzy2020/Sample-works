# ADR-0002: Public scorecard vs private feedback

- **Status:** Proposed
- **Date:**
- **Deciders:**

## Context

Visibility creates pressure. Pressure can motivate, or it can create defensiveness and blame.

## Options considered

### Option 1: Public scorecard ranking teams
- **Pros:** Strong incentive; transparent.
- **Cons:** Shame and gaming; teams with hard services look worst.

### Option 2: Private findings to each team; only aggregate trends public
- **Pros:** Safe; teams engage honestly.
- **Cons:** Less pressure; leadership can't see who needs help.

### Option 3: Public *findings and progress*, no ranking; private detail
- **Pros:** Accountability for follow-through, without league tables.
- **Cons:** Requires careful wording (the finding template).
