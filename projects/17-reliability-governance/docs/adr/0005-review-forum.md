# ADR-0005: Review forum and cadence

- **Status:** Proposed
- **Date:**
- **Deciders:**

## Context

Findings need a regular moment where someone looks, decides and follows up.

## Options considered

### Option 1: Weekly ops review (SRE + team leads, 30 min)
- **Pros:** Fast feedback; small batches.
- **Cons:** Meeting fatigue; attendance drops.

### Option 2: Monthly reliability review with leadership
- **Pros:** Authority in the room.
- **Cons:** Slow; findings go stale.

### Option 3: Async weekly report + monthly review for escalations only
- **Pros:** Low meeting cost; escalations still get attention.
- **Cons:** Async reports are easy to skip.
