# ADR-0005: Who closes the gaps

- **Status:** Proposed
- **Date:**
- **Deciders:**

## Context

SRE can add monitoring quickly, but then SRE owns monitoring for services it doesn't understand.

## Options considered

### Option 1: SRE closes gaps directly
- **Pros:** Fast; consistent quality.
- **Cons:** Teams don't learn; SRE becomes a bottleneck and owns everything.

### Option 2: Owning teams close gaps; SRE provides the standard, templates and review
- **Pros:** Ownership stays where the knowledge is.
- **Cons:** Slower; depends on team priorities (this is where project 17's accountability comes in).

### Option 3: SRE closes tier-1 gaps with the team (pairing), teams close the rest
- **Pros:** Speed where it matters, learning everywhere.
- **Cons:** Needs clear rules on hand-over.
