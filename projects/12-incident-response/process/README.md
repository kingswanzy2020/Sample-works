# Incident process (you write this)

These files are **prompts**, not finished policy. Each one lists the questions your process must answer.
Fill them in from your ADRs, then test them in a game day and revise.

| File | Answers |
|------|---------|
| `severity-levels.md` | What makes something Sev1 vs Sev2 vs Sev3, in business terms? |
| `roles.md` | Who does what during an incident, and how is the incident commander chosen? |
| `communication.md` | Who hears what, when, and through which channel? |
| `on-call.md` | Rotation, handoff, escalation, and what on-call is (and isn't) expected to do |
| `runbook-standard.md` | What every runbook must contain (project 17 measures runbooks against this) |
