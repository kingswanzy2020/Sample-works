# What I'd do differently at a different scale

## Now: one or two people, game days
- Which part of the process made the biggest difference?

## 10×: several teams, real on-call
- Incident tooling (Slack bot / incident.io / PagerDuty) creating channels, roles and timelines automatically.
- Regular training and shadowing for new on-call engineers.

## 100×: firm-wide
- Dedicated incident commanders for major incidents; formal comms to the business and regulators.
- Incident review board looking at patterns (project 17), not single incidents.
