# ADR-0005: Postmortem policy and action tracking

- **Status:** Proposed
- **Date:**
- **Deciders:**

## Context

Postmortems only help if actions get done. Most organisations write the document and forget the actions.

## Options considered

### Option 1: Postmortem for every incident
- **Pros:** Nothing missed.
- **Cons:** Fatigue; low-value documents.

### Option 2: Required for Sev1/Sev2 and repeats; optional otherwise
- **Pros:** Effort goes where it matters.
- **Cons:** Repeats of small incidents may be missed.

### Option 3: Option 2 + structured action tracking with owners and due dates, reviewed weekly
- **Pros:** Accountability (this is what project 17 measures).
- **Cons:** Someone must run the review.

## Questions to answer before deciding

- What makes a postmortem blameless in practice, not just in name?
- What happens to an action that's overdue?
