# What I'd do differently at a different scale

## Now: 8 services, one report
- Which SLO turned out to be the most useful conversation-starter?

## 10×: ~100 services
- SLO specs owned by service teams (in their repos), aggregated centrally.
- Dependency map from traces; attribution automated.

## 100×: firm-wide
- Reliability reviewed alongside business metrics; error budgets influence release decisions.
- Customer-journey SLOs (e.g. "order placed to confirmation") across many services.
